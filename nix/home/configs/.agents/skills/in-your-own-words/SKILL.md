Restate, in your own words, the goal behind the user's requests this session and the problem they are trying to solve: what done looks like for them, and why they want it. Quote the user's sentence each part rests on, and mark any part no sentence of theirs states as your inference. End with the one point you are least sure of, so the user can correct it.
